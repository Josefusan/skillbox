# Peslar channel call — 2026-09-19 (transcript skill)

**Type:** episodic playbook (dated). Prefer live sheet + `ctv-zevari-var-loop` for current ops; use this for channel economics and Peslar alignment.

**Parties:** Joseph Clark (CTV) · John Peslar (Zevari/channel)
**Duration:** ~26 min · Notes via Fireflies

## What was agreed / directional

1. **CTV as VAR + channel:** Joseph recruits BDRs (often underused, verified women’s LinkedIn accounts), hooks them to **Zevari** (+ **Turnwards** for inbound), runs outreach; booked meetings go to Joseph and/or Peslar’s closers; CTV earns kickback/commission on attributed deals.
2. **Inbound > outbound for this offer**, but **do both**. Lead magnets / inbound outperform pure targeted outbound on a mid-ticket product; outbound still adds volume.
3. **Women’s LinkedIn accounts outperform men’s** for engagement/inbound trust.
4. **ICP sweet spot:** **100–500 employees**. Larger enterprise = long cycles (1–3 months) and meeting spam Joseph/Peslar don’t want to staff alone. Joseph may act as **commission-only AE** for those fish.
5. **Stack (LinkedIn Premium + Zevari + Turnwards):** Peslar indicated he would **comp the stack** if Joseph sells **commission-only** — Joseph shouldn’t bake full SaaS seat cost into early ROI if that holds.
6. **Peslar asked Joseph to run the numbers and pitch him** margin/commission structure (Joseph owns the profit model).
